# Review Queue

Spaced retrieval practice. The tutor follows the rules in AGENTS.md ("Spaced repetition"):

- Boxes and intervals: box 1 = next session, box 2 = 3 days, box 3 = 7 days, box 4 = 21 days, box 5 = 60 days.
- Correct (without hints) moves an item up one box; wrong or hinted moves it back to box 1.
- Due = date of last review + interval of the new box. An item answered correctly in box 5 is retired (move it to the Retired table).
- Each warm-up takes up to 5 due items, oldest due first, and mixes at least two modules.
- Add 3-5 new items per session (box 1). Items from `mistakes.md` always enter here.
